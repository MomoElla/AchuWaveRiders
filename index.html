Arugam Bay's Main Point is a long, peeling right-hander — forgiving enough to learn on, good enough that you'll never want to leave.

We're a small crew of local and long-stay instructors who've surfed this coastline for years. Lessons stay small on purpose, so you actually get corrected, not just supervised.

Whether you've never stood on a board or you're chasing your first proper point-break ride, we'll get you reading the wave, not just riding whitewash.
